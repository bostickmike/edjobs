Job Summary
This position is located in Wright, WY. The GEAR UP Wyoming Academic Coach will be responsible for advising/mentoring GEAR UP students in 7-12 grades at Wright Jr./Sr. High school, serving as a point of contact with program participants, including their families. Additional duties include providing monthly programming and resources to help students learn about postsecondary education options, promoting a college-going mind-set and providing academic support. This position will also act as a liaison and represent the NWCCD GEAR UP program at Wright Jr./Sr. High School, maintaining program enrollment and negotiating access and arrangements for programming within the school.

Note: There is opportunity to work in the summer if desired.
Reports To GEAR UP Site Coordinator

About Northern Wyoming Community College District
Gillette College is located in Gillette, WY, the county seat of Campbell County. Located midway between the Big Horn Mountains to the West and the Black Hills of South Dakota to the east. Gillette is a growing, family-friendly community and enjoys a very strong economy due to energy-related industries located there.

Sheridan College is located in historic Sheridan, WY at the foot of the beautiful Big Horn Mountains, minutes away from a variety of outdoor recreational activities. Sheridan is ranked “America’s Top Western Town” by True West magazine and is a vibrant community. Sheridan College is a vital component in the community.

NWCCD also includes a small campus site in Buffalo, WY.

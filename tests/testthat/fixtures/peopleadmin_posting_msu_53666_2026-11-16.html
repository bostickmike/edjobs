Brief Position Overview
This position is located in Miles City, MT. The primary purpose of this position is to assist in the conduct of research in the scientific fields of reproductive physiology, cattle nutrition, and rangeland management. The position will also assist the crews responsible for the care and management of livestock. This position’s primary responsibility will be to control the spread of noxious weeds in both rangeland and cropland settings as well as controlling prairie dog populations in rangeland pastures. Additional duties will include rebuilding and repairing all fences on Fort Keogh. Night calving heifers at the horse barn from March until April.

Position Details

General Statement
As an employee of MSU-Ag Experiment Station-Fort Keogh, you will work alongside other MSU employees and USDA employees conducting research in livestock and range management.
Duties and Responsibilities
Primary Duties of this position included:
  • Night Calving (March – April) – Recognize and administer obstetrical assistance in difficult births and other problems associated with calving cattle and have knowledge to know when to call for assistance from the veterinarian when the condition warrants.
  • Building and repairing cattle fences.
  • Rangeland noxious weed control using field sprayer mounted in pickup or on a 4-wheeler.
  • Rangeland prairie dog control using an applicator mounted on a 4-wheeler.


Secondary Duties as needed/when time allows:
  • Snow removal, cleaning ditches, irrigation, operate haying machinery, haul irrigation pipe, change water, and putting out supplements or feed for cattle.
  • Assist cow crew as needed with working or feeding cattle during calving, branding, weaning and shipping seasons. Tasks may include riding horses and moving/handling cattle in a calm low stress manner from pasture to pasture or in working facilities.
  • Other duties included as assigned
Required Qualifications – Experience, Education, Knowledge & Skills
  1. High school diploma or G.E.D.
  2. Have general knowledge of calving cattle and knowledge of livestock health care.
  3. Basic experience with operation of farm and ranch equipment such as tractors, skid steers, and equipment mounted on them.
  4. Knowledge and/or experience in building and repairing cattle fences and the equipment associated with building fences.
  5. Knowledge of workplace safety procedures.
  6. Knowledge and/or experience of accurate record keeping.
  7. Experience with beef cattle operations.
Preferred Qualifications – Experience, Education, Knowledge & Skills
  1. A state approved Montana (or other entity) pesticide applicator license, along with a working knowledge of pesticides, their application and safe uses.
  2. Knowledge of crop and noxious weed identification.
  3. Experience with both large-scale and handheld sprayers. Experience with sprayer calibrating and maintaining sprayers at the correct volume and pressure.
  4. Experience accurately recording, filing, and monitoring detailed records.
  5. Knowledgeable in assessing and treating livestock ailments.
The Successful Candidate Will
  • Have great communication skills and ability to work as part of a team.
  • Exercise initiative and independent judgement in day to day activities.
  • Have the ability to prioritize duties, as well as possess time management skills. .
Position Special Requirements/Additional Information
  • This position is required to obtain a pesticide operator license within 6 months of hire. MSU will pay certification/recertification fees.
  • Must possess currently or be eligible to obtain a valid State of Montana Driver’s license and have an acceptable driving record (less than 12 points).


This job description should not be construed as an exhaustive statement of duties, responsibilities or requirements, but a general description of the job. Nothing contained herein restricts Montana State University’s rights to assign or reassign duties and responsibilities to this job at any time.
Physical Demands
With or without reasonable accommodation; the person in this position must be able to:

  • Maneuver parts, equipment, and livestock that weigh up to 150 lbs.
  • Work in extreme temperatures and inclement weather conditions.
  • Navigate on slippery or uneven terrain.
  • Work with and around pesticides and dust-born particles.
  • Work with and around hazardous machinery that is extremely loud.
  • Handle and work with unpredictable livestock that can cause serious injury.


To perform this job successfully, an individual must be able to perform each essential duty satisfactorily with or without reasonable accommodations. The requirements listed above are representative of the knowledge, skill, and/or ability required.
